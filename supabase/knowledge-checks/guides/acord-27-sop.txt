Standard Operating Procedure
November 17, 2026
Complete the ACORD 27 Evidence of Property
Insurance form
How to fill in each section of the ACORD 27 Evidence of Property Insurance, a single-page
document that shows coverage on residential, commercial, or contents property. Completing it
correctly saves time and money and helps verify coverage details with the insurer.
For: Insurance agency staff (producers and account managers) who issue Evidence of Property documents
From the video: U4hptJxdASYsYTy Steps: 14
WHEN TO USE THIS
When a client, lender, or other party needs proof of property coverage on an ACORD 27 form
BEFORE YOU START
A blank ACORD 27 Evidence of Property Insurance form (the EOP template)
The agency's name and contact information
The Insured's name and the policy and carrier details
The property address and description, the coverages, amounts, and deductibles
The name and address of the additional interest (for example the lender) and its loan number
STEPS
1 Enter the issue date
In the DATE (MM/DD/YYYY) box at the top right, enter the date the insurance agency created the
document.
2 Enter the producer
In the AGENCY box, enter the name of the insurance agency or broker that issued this certificate.

3 Enter the producer's contact information
Fill in the agency's contact details (PHONE, FAX, E-MAIL ADDRESS) so the holder can reach the agent
with questions or to request revisions.
Tip: Not all agents will work with third parties for revisions.
4 Enter the Insured
In the Insured box, enter the first named Insured: the person or company who purchased the insurance
policy.
Tip: The first named Insured has important rights and obligations, including paying the premium,
receiving notice of cancellation, adjusting claims, and making policy changes.

5 Enter the insurance carriers
In the COMPANY box, list the carriers that provide the insurance policies for the Insured.
6 Enter the property location and description
Under PROPERTY INFORMATION, in LOCATION/DESCRIPTION, enter the address of the location and a
description of the building or property.
Important: If the proof of coverage is for something other than a building, describe it in great
detail. For machinery or equipment, include the year, make, model, and serial number.

7 Describe the coverage
Under COVERAGE INFORMATION, in COVERAGE / PERILS / FORMS, enter a description of the coverage
provided and the causes of loss (for example special, broad, or basic).
8 Enter the amount of insurance
In the AMOUNT OF INSURANCE column, enter the amount of insurance for each coverage.

9 Enter the deductible
In the DEDUCTIBLE column, enter the deductible for each coverage.
10 Add remarks
In REMARKS (Including Special Conditions), enter any additional comments or special conditions on the
policy.

11 Enter the additional interest's name and address
Under ADDITIONAL INTEREST, in NAME AND ADDRESS, list the company you are doing business with
that has a financial interest in the property.
12 Check the additional interest type
In the check boxes next to the name and address, check the box for the type of additional interest:
Additional Insured, Lenders Loss Payable, Loss Payee, Mortgagee, or Other (an interest other than
these).

13 Enter the loan number
In LOAN #, enter the account number or other controlling number the additional interest has assigned
the Insured.
14 Sign as authorized representative
In AUTHORIZED REPRESENTATIVE, put the name of the authorized representative.
Important: A name must be in this box for the certificate to be authorized and accepted.
NOTES
The ACORD 27 is a crucial part of a property insurance claim and should not be completed without
consulting an experienced professional.
This guide covers the ACORD 27 only; there are other types of Evidence of Property forms.

The form version shown has check boxes for Mortgagee, Loss Payee, and Additional Insured; the
other interest types may appear on other versions of the form.
TERMS
ACORD 27: The Evidence of Property Insurance form
EOP: Evidence of Property
Producer: The insurance agency or broker that issued the certificate
First named Insured: The person or company who purchased the policy; pays the premium and
receives notice of cancellation
Additional interest: A company with a financial interest in the property, such as a lender
