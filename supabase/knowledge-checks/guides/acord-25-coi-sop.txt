# ACORD 25 — Certificate of Insurance (COI) Request Procedure (from "COI Request Processing SOP.pdf"). Text only; the PDF's screenshots are HawkSoft screens with client details blacked out.
Standard Operating Procedure & Step-by-Step Workflow Guide
Certificate of Insurance (COI) Request Procedure — ACORD 25 Certificate of Liability Insurance, issued from HawkSoft.

WHEN TO USE THIS
When a client (the insured) or a vendor asks for a Certificate of Insurance (COI) showing their liability coverage
for a certificate holder (for example a general contractor, landlord, or a state licensing board).

STEPS
1 Open the Client File. Make a note depending on the type of COI requested.
  (The client's Logs tab shows earlier activity, e.g. "Email > To > Other > Print Or Create: Certificate of Liability (025)" tagged "Issued COI".)
2 Go to Action - Email - To-Insured. Paste the content of the email request received.
  (This opens Create Log; the activity tag used is "Issued COI".)
3 In the same log notes, go to New Action - Mail/Email - To-Insured - Print Create.
4 Continue the same process and select Certificate of Liability (ACORD 25).
  (The Print/Create list also offers Evidence of Property (27), Certificate of Property (24), Vehicle Certificate, ACORD Forms, etc.)
5 Double click on the Master Certificate List to see the rest of the options.
6 Check each tab to see that the policies are not outdated or expired.
  (Tabs: Terms, Producer & Insured, General Liability, Automobile Liability, Excess Liability, Workers Comp Liability,
  Other 1, Other 2, Description of Operations Template, Document Library, Certificate Holder. The Terms tab shows the
  form edition, ACORD 25 (2016/03).)
7 Go to Certificate Holder and search for the name of the vendor / certificate holder. If not available, click Add to fill out the details.
8 Double check the address, city, state, and zip code of the certificate holder.
9 Click the box and add the specific Description of Operations based on how the client likes to format the template.
  If included, all boxes can be checked too (Include Vehicle Schedule / Driver List / Location List on ACORD 101;
  Additional Remarks use the ACORD 101 Additional Remarks Schedule).
10 Click on the specific policy (section) requested by the certificate holder: General Liability, Automobile Liability,
  Excess Liability, Workers Comp Liability, Other 1, Other 2. If there is an Additional Insured, check that box;
  the same goes for Subrogation Waived.
11 After clicking OK, the screen goes back to the main page. Click OK and the print option shows up
  ("Print, Email or Attach Certificate(s)?"). Click Yes.
12 The screen automatically selects/checks the Certificate Holder. Click OK.
13 Select Email or Print to send the Certificate of Insurance. Click Print Preview to see how the certificate will look.
  Make sure to select the Signature of Agent. Click Finish.
  Note: after clicking Finish, make sure to log notes.

KEY CHECKS
- Never issue a certificate with an expired or outdated policy: check every tab first (step 6).
- The certificate holder's name and full address must be correct (steps 7-8).
- Only mark ADDL INSD (Additional Insured) and SUBR WVD (Subrogation Waived) when the policy actually provides it
  and the holder requested it (step 10).
- Always select the agent's signature (step 13) and log the activity afterwards.
